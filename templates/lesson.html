Lesson 1

Our Solar System

Explore the Sun, planets, and the patterns that shape days, nights, and years.

Reading material

Our solar system has the Sun at the center, with 8 planets orbiting around it: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. The four planets closest to the Sun - Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars - are small and rocky. The four farther out - Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune - are much bigger and made mostly of gas.

Earth is special because it's the only planet we know of with liquid water, breathable air, and living things. Earth spins on its axis once every 24 hours - that's why we have day and night - and it takes 365 days to travel all the way around the Sun, which is one year.

Fun fact: A day on Venus is longer than its year! Venus takes 243 Earth days to spin once, but only 225 Earth days to orbit the Sun.
